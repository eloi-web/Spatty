# Spatty - Your Images in Sphere

Spatty is an immersive, interactive 3D gallery built with React, Three.js, and MediaPipe. Explore your photos in a floating sphere or a tunnel view using your mouse or hand gestures. Look like you're diving into the future and navigating the digital world just with your hands!

## Features

- **Spatial Sphere View**: Experience a dense, majestic floating sphere of photos.
- **Through View**: Fly through an infinite runner tunnel of your uploaded images.
- **Hand Gesture Navigation**:
  - `✌️ + Drag` to rotate the sphere (Index and Middle fingers extended).
  - `🤏 Pinch` distance (Index + Thumb) to zoom in and out of the gallery.
  - `🤙 Shaka sign` to toggle the Fullscreen mode.
- **Traditional Controls**: Simply click and drag to rotate, or scroll to zoom in/out with the mouse wheel.

## Getting Started

Upload your images directly in the UI clicking the **Upload Gallery** button, jump right in and enjoy the immersive experience through either mouse interactions or your webcam.

### Tech Stack
- React
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- Three.js (@react-three/fiber and @react-three/drei)
- MediaPipe (Hand Tracking)
